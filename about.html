Our Story

Founded at the heart of a quiet Italian district, Savorè began with a simple belief — that food should never just fill the stomach… it should ignite the senses, awaken the soul, and bring people together. What started as a small family kitchen serving aged steaks and handcrafted pastas has grown into one of the city’s most refined culinary destinations. Every plate we serve carries decades of tradition, passion, and the unmistakable artistry of authentic Italian cooking. At Savorè, we don’t just cook. We curate moments — slow, rich, indulgent moments that stay with you long after the last bite. Our chefs are artisans. Every dish is treated like a masterpiece — measured, balanced, precise, and plated with intention. This dedication is why Savorè has earned the trust of thousands of guests who return for the same unmistakable quality: bold flavors, memorable service, and a dining experience crafted with heart.

What Makes Us Different

We honor tradition, embrace innovation, and pour genuine passion into every flavor, texture, and detail. FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions About Savorè

What Type Of Meals do you serve at Savorè?

We serve local Italian dishes and Foreign Continentals depending on preferences, Bookings and Reservations. Our Main dishes are steak, meat balls, Italian Pasta, Alfredo Cuisines, Emino Steaks etc. Check Our Menu Page to Place an order.

Do you have a kids' menu available?

No. Unfortunately, Kids menu are not included in our food menus and catalogues but, you can make a special request in the text area of the reservation form stating what kind of food you would like to get for your ward.

Do I need a reservation before I can visit?

Yes. At Savorè, Food Is Dine-In-Only. We focus on plate artistry, service, presentation and a fancy experience. After Dining in at our residence, you can order a take away.

Do you have a dress code?

We don't actually have a color combination of how dresses should be worn but At Savorè, we prefer formal attires. Anything formal, Decent and Eye Appealing is allowed at Savorè. Any Informal dressing won't be accepted.

What Are your Opening and Closing Hours

Savorè Operates From Thursdays to Sundays from 4:00PM - 12:30AM.

How does payment Systems work here?

You can pay for your meals, orders, wines, and take aways with your bank card, mobile transfers or cheques.
